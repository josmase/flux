## Approved decisions and remaining gates

- User approved the classifications on 2026-11-12 and explicitly excluded all active NFS mounts from backup. This creates the documented exception that historical NFS content has no backup restore path.
- User approved the cache rebuild tests; the Immich and pinned voice-model PVCs remain protected until their selected data can be rebuilt and the 24-hour RTO is measured.
- Registry objects are excluded as rebuildable based on the user's 2026-11-10 confirmation. Track Registry metadata recovery separately.
- Keep Prometheus history protected at standard tier and GitLab Valkey protected until its queue-drain/rebuild or native restore test passes.

These decisions do not enable new producers or authorize pruning existing artifacts.
